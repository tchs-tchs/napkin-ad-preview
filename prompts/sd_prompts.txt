# Stable Diffusion (Automatic1111 / SDXL) prompts

通用设置建议：
- 纵向尺寸：1080x1920
- Sampler: DPM++ 2M Karras
- Steps: 28-40 (macro 用 32-40)
- Guidance/CFG: 7.5-9
- 使用 Real-ESRGAN 放大（如需）以及 GFPGAN 修复人脸

统一 negative prompt:
lowres, watermark, text, logo (unless provided), deformed, cartoon, extra limbs, oversaturated, jpeg_artifact, blurry, out_of_frame, bad_anatomy, hands_wrong
